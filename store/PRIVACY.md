# Privacy policy: Lift Log for Liftosaur

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Garmin watch app lets you log Liftosaur workouts from your watch. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What the app sends, and where

- **Your Liftosaur API key**, which you enter in the app settings in Garmin Connect. The app sends it only to liftosaur.com, to sign in to your Liftosaur account.
- **Workout data**: the sets you log (reps, weight, completion time), workout start and end times, and pauses. The app sends it only to liftosaur.com, through the Garmin Connect app on your phone.
- **A random device ID** created by the app, sent to liftosaur.com with each change so Liftosaur can tell your devices apart.
- **The app's name and version** (for example "liftosaur-garmin/1.0.0"), sent with each change.

If sets are still waiting when you close the app, the watch sends them in the background, at most every 5 minutes, until they are sent, the same way and to the same place.

The developer of this app runs no server and receives none of this data. Liftosaur handles the data under its own privacy policy: https://www.liftosaur.com/privacy.html

## What stays on the watch

- The current workout and its rest timer, until the workout ends.
- Sets that have not been sent yet, until they are sent. Sets Liftosaur refused are kept (at most 200) so you can resend them from the app's menu.
- The name of the next or ongoing workout day, for the glance and for watch faces that show the app's complication.
- The last sync error, until you retry.
- The device ID, for as long as the app is installed.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Garmin activity

The app records a Strength Training activity (heart rate, calories, time, and one lap per set with the exercise name, its Liftosaur and Garmin exercise ids, reps, weight and whether it was a warmup, plus the program name, day name, total sets and total volume) on your watch. Garmin Connect syncs it like any other activity, under Garmin's privacy policy.

## No tracking

The app has no ads, analytics or tracking, and it shares no data with anyone other than Liftosaur and Garmin as described above.
